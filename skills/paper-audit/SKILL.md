# Paper Audit

Read the current manuscript and relevant evidence. Check claim/evidence alignment, numbers, terminology, equations and meaningful transitions. Use `scripts/manuscript_audit.py` or revision tools where helpful. A paragraph ledger, fixed background map or every-symbol form is not mandatory. Correct substantive drift and preserve useful uncertainty. Detector scores cannot prove authorship or justify changing scientific meaning.

Follow the current research-first principles in `AGENTS.md`; use this capability whenever it helps the task. Historical stage labels are compatibility context, not prerequisites.

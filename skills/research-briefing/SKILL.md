# Research Briefing

Organize the presentation around the question, contribution, decisive evidence and useful next decisions. Use supplied paper/source material and an appropriate slide tool; any capable model may write text and figures. Preserve source references and distinguish proposed from observed work. Inspect the rendered slides. Slide count and author-plan forms are choices for the audience, not fixed scientific requirements.

Follow the current research-first principles in `AGENTS.md`; use this capability whenever it helps the task. Historical stage labels are compatibility context, not prerequisites.

# Paper Analyze

Inspect the available original sections, methods, figures and results needed for the question. State the actual reading scope and distinguish author statements from your inference. Optional cards and the existing literature workbench can preserve sources and notes; filling a card does not establish reading. Compare relevant assumptions, evidence and unresolved contradictions without completing every phase or waiting for a stage decision.

Follow the current research-first principles in `AGENTS.md`; use this capability whenever it helps the task. Historical stage labels are compatibility context, not prerequisites.

# Paper Reader

Use the actual PDF/text and appropriate extraction or page-render tools. Read rather than infer unseen sections from the abstract. Preserve physical page/printed-label distinctions and state inaccessible or unassessed material. Translation can help comprehension but does not become the original evidence. See `references/research-methods/paper-cards.md`; no card or source-scope ceremony is needed before reading available material.

Follow the current research-first principles in `AGENTS.md`; use this capability whenever it helps the task. Historical stage labels are compatibility context, not prerequisites.

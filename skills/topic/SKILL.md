# Topic intelligence

Read the Topic intelligence contract, `references/research-integrity.md`, and the project constraints.

Coordinate three independent workstreams:

- literature frontier, unresolved contradictions, benchmark saturation and enabling technologies;
- degree/admission pathways and funded-position demand in the selected countries;
- industry demand, salary proxies, skill adjacency and five-year durability.

Require official/current sources for regulations, positions and market facts, and primary literature for scientific claims. Record queries, dates, result counts and exclusions.

Produce at least three serious candidate programs. For each, test novelty, doctoral depth, paperability, public-data feasibility, compute/equipment ceiling, competition, supervisor/position supply, job-market breadth and failure modes.

Define:

- proposition A: one coherent doctoral-level core;
- proposition B: a valuable extension that could independently support a second doctoral-level program, not merely an extra experiment;
- a six-paper draft map, used only as a feasibility test before G2.

Use Claude judgments only as internal semantic plans. Ask Codex/OpenAI to write the persistent topic artifacts in independent wording, then use a different model family to attack the preferred choice. Resolve objections explicitly and stop at G1.

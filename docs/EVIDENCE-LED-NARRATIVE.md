# Evidence-led narrative and focused comparison

Native user-requested policy, 2026-10-18. This extends the reviewed
anti-defensive-writing adaptation and the existing G2-G5 contracts within the
current model calls, evidence records, budgets and human decisions.

## Preserve one doctoral topic across all six papers

The six papers must cumulatively answer one overarching doctoral question. Each
paper has a distinct subquestion, independently sufficient evidence and standalone
value, with an explicit role in the integrated thesis. Core A and independently
evidenced extension B remain within that same doctoral topic; scientific
independence does not create permission to pursue unrelated topics.

Establish the common question and A/B relationship at G1. At G2, use the existing
paper contracts, paper-map roles/dependencies and `thesis_synthesis` to explain
each paper's contribution and the cumulative knowledge gained by the collection.
Shared datasets, code or vocabulary alone do not establish this connection.

Evidence-led reframing at G4/G5 or in reviewer revisions must preserve the approved
doctoral scope and paper-to-thesis relationship. If stronger evidence calls for a
substantive change, present the affected G1/G2 records for human review before
dependent work continues, keeping all six papers in one coherent doctoral topic.
Do not silently substitute a different topic or duplicate another paper's claim.

## Build one clear scientific argument

Organize the paper around the strongest supported contribution:

1. A problem that matters to the intended reader.
2. The exact new capability, method, finding, explanation or theoretical result.
3. The advantage established under stated conditions and relevant comparisons.
4. The decisive evidence that makes the contribution credible.
5. The scientific or practical value that follows from that evidence.

Give central claims, main figures and their supporting reasoning the most
attention. Supporting details earn space by their contribution to this argument.
Equal coverage of every implementation choice, metric or completed activity is
not a writing objective. A sequence of experiments is not itself the argument.
During planning, anticipated advantages remain hypotheses; measured advantages
are stated only after evidence exists.

State advantages explicitly: name the comparator, relevant conditions, measured
effect or established property, and uncertainty where applicable. Efficiency,
accuracy, interpretability, robustness and generalization are different claims;
claim only the dimensions actually established.

## Choose a meaningful comparison scope before results

Select dimensions from the research question and intended use. Explain why they
matter, identify relevant competitive alternatives, and match data access,
tuning and resource conditions. A paper need not compete on every imaginable
dimension. The existing simple, domain-standard and strong-recent baseline
requirements continue to apply to the declared empirical scope.

Document the rationale before observing the comparison results. A later
restriction is a change in scope, not permission to erase the original test.
Keep original scoring, outcomes and analysis phase. A mixed result can establish
a measured tradeoff under matched conditions; a weaker metric alone is not a
verdict on the whole method. Tradeoffs do not establish superiority on an
unstated composite metric.

## Give each experiment a job

An experiment should establish the central value, discriminate a mechanism or
alternative explanation, validate a required condition, or define useful scope.
Record its question, relevant claim IDs and expected contribution in the
existing design explanations and argument-plan purpose fields. Keep registered
runtime role enums and baseline assignments unchanged.

Before execution, remove or redesign irrelevant or redundant planned work
through the normal protocol and approval route. Do not spend on experiments
merely to increase table count or satisfy an imagined universal competition.
A proposed additional experiment must identify the actual claim it could
resolve. Distinguish this need from an optional extension.

After execution, prioritize or condense presentation by the declared role,
scientific relevance and adequacy. Registered outcomes remain locatable; primary
tests, falsification tests, required baselines and interpretation-changing
findings remain visible in the main text. Cross-reference supporting detail.
Unfavorable direction or controversy alone cannot justify deletion, demotion,
altered scoring or a weaker comparator.

## Let the story follow the strongest evidence

Reassess the central claim when results arrive. A well-supported bounded
contribution, tradeoff or informative boundary may be more valuable than the
originally anticipated narrative. Rebuild the argument around that contribution
instead of defending an unsupported original story.

Keep the original hypothesis, protocol, outcomes and actual analysis phase.
Exploratory or post-hoc findings remain identified as such. Reframing does not
make a discovery prospectively confirmed, establish a mechanism from a numeric
threshold, or authorize another experiment.

Use existing records rather than adding a parallel narrative store:

| Existing record | Narrative use |
|---|---|
| paper-contract question, contribution and originality boundary | Planned problem, value and intended scope |
| claim-evidence matrix and paper-facts claim_alignment | Current supported claim, inference strength, evidence and section wording |
| research-increments arguments: purpose, claim_ids, inference_boundary, evidence and precedence | Central/supporting paragraph roles, evidence order and changed emphasis |
| revision ledger and itemized decisions | What changed, why, source locations and retained outcomes |

Use only schema-supported fields. Preserve historical plans; a changed argument
plan needs the ordinary pre-write freeze for the next writing pass. Changes to
scientific meaning, contracted scope or frozen inputs follow the existing
revision/authorization route. Completed confirmatory protocols remain frozen.

## Keep scientific review useful and manuscript prose focused

Internal reviewers test the actual central claim against evidence and classify
issues as demonstrated problems, verification questions or optional extensions.
They retain independent judgment and material findings. A missing experiment
matters for the precise inference it would support, not an imagined broader paper.

The manuscript states the supported contribution, clear advantages and necessary
conditions. It incorporates issues that affect interpretation and provides
evidenced responses where required. It need not copy a brainstorm of speculative
objections, generic self-criticism or repeated caveats.

The conclusion reinforces the established contribution, decisive evidence and
bounded value. Keep an interpretation-critical qualifier beside its claim.
Introduce no new result, universal superiority claim, experiment diary or
catalogue of optional limitations.

These instructions reuse the existing planner, writer, remediation and
independent review paths. The original per-group editorial dispositions,
evidence/statistical checks, two fresh reviews and human gates remain in force.

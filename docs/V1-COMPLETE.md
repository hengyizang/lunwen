# Doctoral Research OS v1.0 — Quality Policy

The OS is deliberately stricter than a generic paper-writing agent. A project cannot advance from G1 through G5 unless the current gate has a machine-validated quality report.

## Hard floors

- Every scored dimension: **>= 8.0/10**.
- Overall gate score: **>= 8.5/10**.
- Novelty: at least **3 dated primary evidence items** and a comparative literature/patent basis; an "I could not find it" claim is not accepted as novelty evidence.
- G1: each serious direction must have at least **two current Q1 SCI/SCIE target venues**.
- G2: every paper must have its own falsifiable contribution and at least **two Q1 SCI/SCIE targets**; salami slicing is a hard failure.
- G3: the experiment must be capable of supporting a Q1-level scientific claim with baselines, ablations, leakage controls, uncertainty/statistics, reproducibility and feasible compute/data constraints.
- G4: observed evidence must support the claims at Q1-reviewer strength; failed and negative runs cannot be hidden.
- G5: the selected venue must be **current JCR Q1, SCI/SCIE, IF > 1.0**, and two adversarial review rounds must have no unresolved fatal findings.

## What this does and does not guarantee

These are **hard readiness floors**, not a mathematical guarantee of acceptance or novelty. JCR quartiles can change by year/category, reviewers can reject for reasons outside the schema, and a model cannot prove that an idea has never existed. The OS therefore requires current primary-source evidence and explicit human approval at every gate.

Automatic journal login, upload and submission remain disabled by design.

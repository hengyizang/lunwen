# CCFA editorial methods in the existing review cycle

Pinned Humanizer, anti-defensive and CCFA methods remain available to the existing
writer and independent reviewer. The separate H/AD/CFH mechanical scanners,
coverage tables, per-ID disposition parser and standalone reports are retired.
No equivalent detector result or completed semantic review is claimed.

`scripts.ccfa_runtime.humanization_contract` supplies the common evidence and
role boundaries. Ordinary writing requests receive compact applicability
pointers. An explicit `ccf-humanization` or `full-reference-review` request adds
the complete native Humanizer guidance and legitimate-use exceptions, the
anti-defensive groups/checklist/procedure/sentence functions, and the selected
original CCFA policy bodies within the same authorized model call. Full source
text that was not supplied is never described as consumed.

The originals remain pinned under `third_party/humanizer`,
`third_party/anti-defensive-writing` and `third_party/ccfa-skills`.
`integrations/editorial-upstreams.lock.json` and the existing CCFA resource lock
bind their actual bytes. Native adaptations identify their current configuration
hashes. Missing or changed optional H/AD sources are reported as unavailable;
they do not introduce another scientific gate or paid request.

The method guides address direct scientific arguments, calibrated uncertainty,
legitimate contrasts and technical terms, paragraph purpose, relevant comparisons,
material limitations and interpretation of adverse results. Code, quotations,
equations, numeric/claim/citation bindings and actual source evidence stay under
the existing A integrity checks. Review-only roles never write persistent prose.
Methods and examples do not supply new facts, results or scientific authority.

The remaining generic academic-style/linter report uses schema 1.3. Its
`editorial_methods` explicitly records `status: retired`,
`mechanical_review_performed: false` and `semantic_review_status: unknown`.
A grammar observation is not scientific clearance. Historical H/AD/CFH reports
remain protected history and are not reused as a current pass. The legacy
`ccfa_humanization` operation returns a nonpaid retired result without writing a
replacement report. Optional wording suggestions belong in advisory uncertainty;
missing style tables or scores cannot require repair, extra review or approval.
Actual scientific findings retain their normal source-bound treatment.

Scientific text, Word citation fields, formulae, units and local comparator
signatures now share `revision_integrity`; rendering and figure-source seals
share `figure_provenance`. These A/B protections remain active independently of
any editorial method. Manuscript hygiene still preserves originals and change
receipts for the deterministic character cleanup; it does not establish AI
provenance or statistical-watermark absence.

The current user choices override conflicting historical source prose:
licence/research-use checks are optional metadata; source/version, integrity,
privacy and known access restrictions remain. Complete negative/failed/registered
comparisons are backed up separately under `experiments/archive`; manuscripts
select scientifically relevant evidence and retain interpretation-changing facts.
Public AI-use statements are optional and author-editable; internal provenance
remains truthful. Model assignments, cumulative budgets, actual G1/G2 topic
choices, frozen execution and the prohibition on automatic submission are unchanged.

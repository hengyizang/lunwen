# Masked planning findings

This is a model-assisted masked review of five synthetic proposals, not human validation. Only the supplied planning text was reviewed; no models or tests were executed. Numerical effects are targets, not results. These notes do not rank general model ability.

Across all proposals, session centering is a distinct representation mechanism from C's augmentation: it removes a common additive log-power offset under an ideal scalar gain model. That algebraic invariance alone does not establish that fault information survives centering or that gain caused an observed performance difference. Adding magnitude back is an informative predictive control, not causal identification. Removing explicit IDs also does not remove site information embedded in spectra. Site-label counts are unknown and cannot be assumed to support every split, per-site metric, weighting rule, or bootstrap stratum. A confidence interval crossing zero establishes neither a null effect nor equivalence.

**P1**

- **Strengths:** Clear representation-versus-augmentation distinction; device partitions prevent session leakage; accurately restricts inference to new devices at represented sites. Keeps C as the primary comparator, fixes settings before test access, pairs devices, and treats seed runs as dependent measurements. The 15-fit arithmetic is correct; the magnitude-restoration control is distinct from B.
- **Issues:** Equal site allocation does not prevent a pooled balanced-accuracy advantage driven by site prevalence. A 16-device test and sparse site-label cells can produce unstable intervals. C's jitter distribution and range, the classification threshold, and concrete training settings remain unspecified, so baseline strength is not yet assessable. Stopping new launches at 40 wall minutes or 120 CPU-minutes does not bound already running fits; resource warnings do not enforce 8 GiB. Extraction and inference overhead also need accounting.
- **Needed revisions:** Lock a credible jitter range and all aggregation/threshold rules; define feasible split and missing-class fallbacks without inventing counts. Add prespecified within-site comparisons or a site-prior reference and interpret them with uncertainty. Enforce total wall/CPU/memory limits by terminating active work when necessary. Repeated deterministic logistic fits may be omitted with justification; A/B are optional if C remains a strong, matched comparator.

**P2**

- **Strengths:** States the scalar-gain limitation and makes an explicit out-of-range prediction. Retains C, justifies omitting B, includes a site-prior reference, keeps scalers on training folds, and averages seeds before device inference. The stated maximum is 28 development fits + 3 final refits + 5 failed-run replacements = 36; failed attempts count.
- **Issues:** The statements that a CI including zero means augmentation is sufficient or falsifies the distinction are invalid; such outcomes can simply be imprecise. Observed log-power spread cannot automatically be identified with sensor-gain spread. Training-derived stress offsets are training-fitted quantities, despite the claim of no fitted statistics, and need an explicit formula and common evaluation convention. Pooled out-of-fold AUROC compares scores from different fitted models; a fixed-prediction device bootstrap does not capture variation from overlapping training folds or development-based selection. Two test devices per site cannot guarantee usable within-site AUROC. Automatically attributing pooled-only gains to site confounding is too strong.
- **Needed revisions:** Separate exploratory development screening from a locked-test estimand and specify final seed aggregation, stress construction, and decision rules. Report crossing-zero intervals as inconclusive, with practical-equivalence claims requiring a prespecified margin and suitable interval. Clarify whether the 33-fit launch stop leaves the advertised reserve available. Add an explicit memory allocation and hard total wall/CPU termination including preprocessing; per-fit CPU limits and expected timing are insufficient. Apply site-label stratification only where observed counts permit it.

**P3**

- **Strengths:** Median centering tests a real invariance mechanism; device grouping, a locked test, training-only scaling, and D-versus-C pairing are appropriate. The written 16 + 4 + 2 = 22 count is arithmetically correct and below 36.
- **Issues:** D with centering removed is B under matched preprocessing, configuration, seeds, and training. Its extra two fits are redundant when the existing B runs already match; otherwise the mismatch needs explanation. Selecting devices without final labels conflicts with label-stratified allocation unless a separate allocator uses labels solely to lock the split. Eight final devices, two per site, give very limited AUROC inference and potentially undefined per-site scores. Validation rescaling tests algebraic invariance, while the primary final test does not explicitly document a gain shift. C's jitter strength and stress multipliers are unspecified. Failure to observe an ablation disadvantage is not proof of no mechanism.
- **Needed revisions:** Reuse matching B predictions; lock shared tuning budgets, jitter settings, stress factors, final-refit seeds, and the split-allocation procedure. Clarify how selected configurations and final refits relate to the ablation. Prespecify missing-class/resampling handling and treat final uncertainty as conditional on the fitted models and sampled sites. A log-loss permutation check answers a different question from AUROC and needs its exchangeability assumption stated. Make the 40-minute/160-CPU-minute/7-GiB stop an enforceable total-work stop, including active jobs and overhead.

**P4**

- **Strengths:** A whole-site holdout and leave-one-site-out development directly challenge site shortcuts. Brier score remains defined even when a held-out site has one label. C and D receive matched seed counts; the magnitude probe and magnitude-restoration control add useful diagnostics. The 27 + 7 + 2 = 36 maximum includes crash replacements.
- **Issues:** One held-out site supports a result conditional on that site, not population-level unseen-site inference. Brier score measures calibration as well as discrimination, and class reweighting changes its calibration target. Unknown site-label counts may make the proposed weights or secondary AUROC unusable. C's jitter range must exclude each development validation site. Inner early stopping must not conceal an additional refit. The stated exact sign-flip test requires a method-exchangeability or symmetry null; zero mean paired difference alone is insufficient. Four approximately 2-GiB workers exhaust the entire memory allowance before shared data and orchestration. A launch at 42 minutes can exceed 45; worker CPU caps also do not cap wall time. The go/no-go rules can disagree because they combine different interval and permutation criteria.
- **Needed revisions:** Lock fold-local jitter and weights with explicit absent-class fallbacks, count any inner refits, and state final early-stopping rules without test access. Match or justify ablation seed asymmetry. Add controlled stress to interpret gain specificity, while retaining natural held-out-site evaluation. State the permutation assumptions and a mutually exclusive decision rule. Reserve memory for overhead and enforce hard total 45-wall/180-CPU-minute/8-GiB limits across feature extraction, workers, inference, and statistics; projected wave timing is not a guarantee.

**P5**

- **Strengths:** Clear new-device/later-session estimand, no test adaptation, no tuning, a matched C comparator, and an explicit practical-effect target. Uses devices for paired uncertainty and acknowledges fixed sites and uncertain gain shifts. Missing within-site classes are recognized rather than assumed away.
- **Issues:** B is already the model without centering. The extra B-without-centering ablation duplicates three existing B fits under the stated matched setup: 12 distinct fits suffice, although 15 attempted fits would remain within the cap. Later-session evaluation combines temporal change and device generalization; it does not establish a gain-specific effect. Pooled AUROC remains vulnerable to site shortcuts. An undefined per-site AUROC does not automatically invalidate an otherwise defined pooled estimand, although it limits confound assessment. The primary treatment of three seeds is unspecified. Projected CPU use does not enforce the actual total CPU cap.
- **Needed revisions:** Reuse B, state whether seed predictions are ensembled before scoring, and define a numerical meaning for a material site reversal. Prespecify pooled and within-site roles and feasible class-coverage/resampling fallbacks. Add evaluation-only earlier-session and/or fixed scalar-stress comparisons to assess the gain interpretation without fitting on test devices. Make the 45-minute stop explicitly terminate active work and enforce cumulative CPU and memory limits including overhead. Treat incomplete matched comparisons as inconclusive, as already proposed.

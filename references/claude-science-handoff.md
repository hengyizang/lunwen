# Importing research platform material

Use the actual supported export or API; do not invent a Claude Science interface. Material from Claude or another platform may be reused in research and writing after the relevant source/claim checks, without a vendor authorship restriction.

For a structured export, the existing `schemas/science-evidence.schema.json` and `evidence/claude-science/<bundle-id>/` layout remain available. Retain source identifiers, original text or permitted local references, page/section anchors, search context and unresolved contradictions. A simple supplied document does not require a bundle merely to be read.

Resolve important citations to actual sources, check quoted/paraphrased support and record inaccessible or partial scope. Source identity and generated explanations are distinct. Preserve actual assistance and avoid treating a platform-generated conclusion as independently verified evidence.

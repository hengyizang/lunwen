# Paper reading and optional cards

Method sources: nature-skills, kdense-topic-methods.

Read an available PDF, publisher text or supplied excerpt and describe the actual scope: full text, abstract, excerpt or metadata. A filename, DOI, extraction or registered source map does not establish that the whole paper was read. Keep usable original page/block locations, formulas, tables and figure context; never invent missing pages after OCR fails.

An optional `evidence/paper-cards/<paper-id>.json` can use `schemas/paper-card.schema.json` to capture the question, author contribution, methods, evidence and candidate ideas. Separate author statements from your analysis, and mark unassessed sections honestly. The card format is a navigation aid, not a prerequisite to reading or writing.

A useful candidate grows from a located assumption, contradiction, limitation or unexplained observation. State its prediction, difference from known work and a test that could change the judgment. Consider plausible failure explanations without a fixed count. Generic improvement language and search absence do not establish originality.

Use `scripts/research_candidates.py paper-card` when the structured format helps. Source-scope records may bind actual supplied files through `scripts/source_scope.py record_registered`; they confirm supplied scope, not human reading or scientific verification. Keep the original evidence available and qualify claims according to what was actually inspected.

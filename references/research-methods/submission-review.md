# Evidence-anchored submission questions

Primary review method: K-Dense peer-review scientific workflow, unchanged source
at third_party/kdense/skills/peer-review/SKILL.md, pinned in
integrations/vendored-research-methods.json. The existing native writing critic
receives its scope, neutral claim map, claim/evidence, methods/statistics,
reproducibility, figure/table and actionable-comment passages directly through
research_methods.stage_context; final submission preparation also supplies them
to the existing writer/planner. Missing view material remains unassessed.
Only these scientific passages are selected. Upstream intake, licence/research-use
and venue AI-policy gates are superseded by the current owner policy; no upstream
script, extra paid pass, numerical acceptance threshold or new C gate is added.
Public AI-use statements remain author-editable. Complete registered outcomes
stay in the separate experiment archive; the manuscript need not reproduce all
comparisons. Existing review prompts bind the primary method's commit, hash and passages.

Supplementary method sources: academic-research-skills, ars-codex, nature-skills, paperspine.
Based on Academic Research Skills by Cheng-I Wu,
https://github.com/Imbad0202/academic-research-skills .
The retained ARS framework and rubric use CC BY-NC 4.0; see integrations/licenses.

Use config/pre-submission-criteria.json as the pre-submission question set.
Apply seven universal dimensions: originality, methodological rigor, evidence
sufficiency, argument coherence, writing quality, literature integration and
significance. Add the criteria for empirical/computational, theoretical,
review/meta-analysis, case-study or policy work. Use the target venue's current
official criteria and reporting standards where their scope applies.

The seven universal questions and exact question-bank hash enter the compact
native review prompt alongside the K-Dense excerpts. Select `submission-review`,
`config/pre-submission-criteria.json` or `full-method-review` to load the complete
article-type question bank. Its unrequested body is not copied into every call.
Apply the universal questions and the relevant
article-type questions to the supplied sources; missing material stays
unassessed. Record actual findings in the existing independent review with claim,
manuscript and evidence locations, uncertainty and a proportionate resolution
test. No complete dimension-by-dimension form or finding quota is required.

The standalone categorical checklist scanner and its report are retired.
Do not generate reviews/pre-submission-checklist.json or
reviews/pre-submission-review.json. Preserve historical originals, but they
provide no current coverage, semantic judgement or clearance. These questions
remain NOT_CALIBRATED and cannot supply points, totals, acceptance probabilities
or an extra paid pass. A real methodological, source/claim or numeric failure
retains its A status; a missing optional mechanical form creates no blocker.

Ordinary cycles use one current independent critique; actual actionable findings
alone justify repair and fresh re-review. At final submission use the existing
three-family mutually blind panel in references/workflow.md: domain receives
Methods/Results/numeric tables, editorial receives title/abstract/Figure 1/headline,
and statistics receives protocol/numeric tables/figure values. Bind exact hashes
and source ranges; exclude prior reviews and desired verdicts. Freeze all three
responses before controller synthesis, preserving disagreement without votes or
acceptance estimates. Historical dual-review receipts are not a new panel.

Each substantive finding should identify the criterion ID, claim/manuscript
location, evidence location, decision impact, missing evidence and a
proportionate resolution test. Do not impose finding quotas or fabricate
reviewer identities. Audit arithmetic, aggregation, uncertainty, exclusions,
duplicate displays, result placement, source provenance and reproducibility
after the independent reviews. Preserve genuine disagreement.

For each rendered figure, inspect the entire figure and each panel at final
physical size. Apply the pinned Nature panel-alignment and PDF collision
helpers through scripts/figure_layout.py. Expose layout observations without inventing visual inspection or another gate. Actual wrong numbers, missing result provenance or a failed final B render remain blocking. AI imagery cannot substantiate data.

# Evidence-anchored scientific review

Primary method: K-Dense peer-review workflow, retained at `third_party/kdense/skills/peer-review/SKILL.md` and identified in `integrations/vendored-research-methods.json`. Supplementary methods: academic-research-skills, ars-codex, nature-skills, paperspine. Academic Research Skills is by Cheng-I Wu, https://github.com/Imbad0202/academic-research-skills ; retained ARS framework/rubric sources use CC BY-NC 4.0 (see `integrations/licenses`).

Choose questions that matter to the current manuscript: originality, methodological rigor, evidence sufficiency, argument coherence, writing, literature integration and significance. Add appropriate empirical, theoretical, qualitative, review or policy questions. `config/pre-submission-criteria.json` is an optional question bank; it is not a calibrated numerical acceptance model.

Inspect the actual evidence needed to assess a claim. A finding should explain the location, source, consequence and proportionate resolution. Distinguish demonstrated errors, verification questions and optional extensions. Missing material remains unassessed rather than becoming an invented failure or pass.

Use independent perspectives when they resolve uncertainty. The existing blind-panel tool may help a deep review, but no fixed family/count or extra paid round is mandatory. Supply enough context, avoid contaminating an independent review with a desired verdict, and preserve disagreements without taking a vote as truth.

Check arithmetic, uncertainty, exclusions, citation support and the rendered figures or manuscript as relevant to the deliverable. Image inspection must actually occur; an inventory or PDF-text extraction is not visual review. Layout suggestions are advisory, while an incorrect displayed result needs correction.

The old categorical scanner, complete dimension form and H/AD/CFH scorecards are retired. Do not recreate them to clear a task. Preserve original results and historical reviews; full logs may remain in the archive while interpretation-changing evidence stays visible. External submission follows actual user authorization.

# Doctoral and JCR Q1 readiness basis

Accessed: 2026-09-01.

## Interpretation boundary

JCR quartiles rank journals within a JIF category; they are not a universal
experimental-design standard and may differ by category and annual edition.
The workflow therefore requires **current JCR Q1 SCI/SCIE** for candidate and
selected venues and separately enforces scientific-design checks. It never
treats a gate score as proof of novelty, doctoral merit, acceptance or
publication.

## General computational-research floor

The cross-venue floor is based on current primary publication guidance rather
than an invented universal “Q1 checklist”:

- NeurIPS Paper Checklist Guidelines require authors to address reproducibility,
  transparent experimental details, statistical significance or uncertainty,
  compute/resources, limitations, ethics and access to artifacts as applicable:
  https://neurips.cc/public/guides/PaperChecklist
- IEEE Author Center encourages sharing data, code and research outputs so that
  experiments and conclusions can be verified and reproduced:
  https://magazines.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/create-your-ieee-magazine-article/research-reproducibility/
- IEEE Access identifies detailed methodology, data repositories, code
  repositories and executable artifacts as central reproducibility mechanisms:
  https://ieeeaccess.ieee.org/authors/reproducibility/
- Nature Machine Intelligence reporting policies require transparent methods,
  statistics and availability of data/materials/code, and request source data
  tied to figures where applicable:
  https://www.nature.com/natmachintell/editorial-policies/reporting-standards
  and
  https://www.nature.com/natmachintell/submission-guidelines/aip-and-formatting

## Repository consequences

- G1 requires comparative closest-work evidence, adjacent-field searches,
  counterevidence, non-novel components, residual novelty risk, a coherent
  doctoral case and a reliability strategy.
- G2 requires structured contracts, two current JCR Q1 targets per paper and a
  complete pairwise anti-salami matrix.
- G3 requires one or more designs per paper, traceable strong/domain-standard/simple baselines,
  ablations, leakage controls, primary metrics, effect sizes, intervals,
  multiplicity handling, power or precision, repeated seeds for stochastic
  work, robustness, negative controls, external validity and reproducibility.
- G4 requires every attempt and current output hash to match the G3-approved
  plan and every contracted claim to trace to registered analyses.
- G5 requires current human-verified JCR Q1 SCI/SCIE status, venue-specific
  checks, deterministic English validation for all submission text, and local
  figure provenance tied to real experiment outputs.
